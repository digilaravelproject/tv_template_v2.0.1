Android & Windows

  • 1. Ensure your device is connected to the hotel Wi-Fi network.
  • 2. Swipe down to open Quick Settings panel.
  • 3. Tap Cast / Smart View / Wireless Display.
  • 4. Select Hotel TV to start mirroring.
🍎

Apple iPhone, iPad & Mac

  • 1. Connect to the same hotel Wi-Fi network as this TV.
  • 2. Swipe down from top-right to open Control Center.
  • 3. Tap Screen Mirroring (AirPlay).
  • 4. Choose Hotel TV to project your screen.

Press BACK on TV remote to return to Home screen
